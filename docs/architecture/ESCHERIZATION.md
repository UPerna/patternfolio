# Boundary-coupled Escherization

`tiling/escherization@1` accepts one or two simple counterclockwise polygon targets. The admitted modes are one IH41 translation tile, two equal-frequency tiles from a split IH41 region, and thick/thin Penrose P3. Targets can have 3–40 vertices. Target order fixes the two-shape assignment. The metric preserves target handedness. [Escherization](https://doi.org/10.1145/344779.345022) [Dihedral Escherization](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/publications/)

The turning-function metric normalizes arc length, integrates squared angular difference at cyclic alignments, and removes the mean angle to factor out rotation. At each interval between alignment events the squared integral is affine and the rotation-corrected variance is concave, so the minimum occurs at an event. The two-target score uses the maximum of the two distances. The optimizer uses a named deterministic stream and bounded geometric cooling. It retains the best admitted integer control vector and records the baseline, best score, individual residuals and budget. A saved witness replays with one verification evaluation and zero annealing steps. The bounded profile uses a fixed target assignment, declared lattices and fixed control resolution.

The IH41 chart maps the unit cell to a parallelogram with the requested aspect and shear. Its periodic edge functions share integer controls. With normalized coordinates, the displacement is `(x + g(y), y + f(x))`. A product of Lipschitz bounds below one gives a contraction when solving the inverse, so the map preserves the cell partition. The split mode maps one monotone internal seam through the same chart. It splits the seam at the displacement knots and gives the two faces opposite uses of the same path. The periodic path coordinates remain rational until drawing.

P3 uses the active quasivertices in Kaplan’s Figure 4.23. The chart represents the four source displacement components on a bounded rational grid in Q(ζ₂₀), where ζ₂₀ is the 18° rotation. The retained Penrose arrow signatures fix the correspondence of each source cell to the thick or thin prototype. The four fundamental edge pairs are a/g, b/e, c/d and f/h. A bend edit belongs to one pair and is applied to every occurrence with the paired direction reversed. Integer chart points identify the shared endpoints. The original P3 instance remains available alongside the derived cells. [Kaplan thesis](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/other/phd/kaplan_diss_escher_print.pdf)

The finite P3 gate checks positive simple prototype contours, exact opposite side incidence, and absence of an unpaired side in the requested rectangle. The winding of the assembled boundary is constant throughout that rectangle, and an admitted interior witness gives winding one. An independent check integrates clipped areas and probes nearby polygon boundaries. The source Penrose construction and finite derived coverage have distinct scopes. The scaffold display draws the original straight-edged construction from its retained data.

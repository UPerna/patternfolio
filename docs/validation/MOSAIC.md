# Decorative mosaics

The `mosaic/hausner@1` provider implements a bounded CPU variant of Alejo Hausner's *Simulating Decorative Mosaics* (SIGGRAPH 2001), sections 3–6. A finite, uniformly sampled convex panel replaces the hardware depth buffer. Each sample belongs to the site whose locally rotated and anisotropically scaled Manhattan pyramid is lowest. Sites move to the centroids of their assigned samples. The nearest artist-selected segment supplies a tangent direction. Declaration order resolves distance ties within the documented numerical tolerance.

The size and aspect controls vary the slopes of the metric near the artist feature. The exclusion strip temporarily removes samples near that feature, followed by full-domain iterations. The trace records the active phase, sample count, mean dimensionless metric, maximum center motion and empty regions. It reports stationary placement, iteration exhaustion or an entirely masked sample domain. The reported energy can rise as assignments, orientations and the active domain change. Sampling density, site count and budget bound computation.

The packer processes site IDs in order and finds the largest allowed scale at each site by bounded bisection. A rectangle stays inside the convex panel and satisfies a separating-axis grout distance to earlier rectangles. A site is rejected if its minimum scale fails. The order is part of the variant and can influence coverage. Grout fraction is the remaining panel area, including boundary gaps. The user supplies analytic color layers and optional panel and feature polylines. A site samples color from the analytic image independently of the orientation and packing passes. PDF and SVG use filled vector paths for every retained tessera. Saved centers bypass the placement solver and are packed and validated again.

The acceptance fixtures cover an analytic two-tone disk, a curved band with an artist-selected parabola and a convex irregular document panel. The unaligned baseline retains the same sample budget and seed. The independent oracle recomputes assignments, centroid moves, orientation error, feature intersections, polygon overlap and vector paths. The near-feature direction tolerance is numerical and the variant offers bounded finite diagnostics for difficult or under-sampled placement.

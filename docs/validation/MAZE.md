# Image-guided mazes

The `maze/image-guided@1` provider represents a convex cell embedding, primal wall incidences, dual passages, two boundary openings and the unique full-maze route. Every shared wall has two opposite cell incidences. Weighted Kruskal removes walls using the source overlapping priority intervals. The directional chart favors removing horizontal walls so that the remaining walls primarily follow the vertical streamline family. The fixed-bias and unbiased comparisons use the same chart and random draws.

This independent bounded variant of Xu and Kaplan's R09 paper uses analytic orthogonal line families for directional regions and concentric contour samples joined by radial edges for spiral and vortex regions. The vortex profile adds four angular corridors to the concentric bias. The random chart starts with isotropic weights and uses bounded jitter and spring relaxation in place of the source attraction-repulsion postprocess. These profiles use analytic image fields and the caller selects the chart. They omit the paper's interactive feature interpolation and arbitrary multi-region editor.

The tone mapping follows section 6.1. It remaps the input lightness into the printable range, then solves for spacing and line width with one active minimum. The chart normalizes spacing to its finite panel and the rendered wall thickness is capped by measured clearance to the potential route segments in nearby cells. The receipt separates requested spacing and width from achieved geometry. The directional chart can be constructed in a rectified plane and then projected by a bounded homography, a reproducible synthetic form of the perspective step in section 6.2.

The route guide supports one simple sequence of waypoints. It snaps them to cells, builds disjoint successive paths through their graph, and pins their openings before completing the tree. This bounded single-route profile does not implement the paper's multi-label distance-matrix routing. A repeated or inaccessible waypoint fails. The solution overlay is reconstructed from the solved route, passing through each opening midpoint. Cropped views are display-only portions of the full maze.

The holed and aperiodic fixtures are original platform extensions. The holed chart removes selected cells and includes a boundary notch. The aperiodic chart takes complete rhombi from the verified P3 scaffold and preserves their cell and vertex identities. The independent checker reconstructs openings from the exported vector wall geometry, solves the exported graph, verifies line widths and route clearance, and compares tone and routing effects with the baseline. Stage 24 depends on this gate. The maze acceptance fixtures remain part of subsequent CLI, browser, export and release verification.
